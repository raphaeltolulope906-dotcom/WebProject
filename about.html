About Us

Supersaver Supermarket is a Nigerian supermarket chain that started in 2013 and operates several branches across Lagos. It was founded with the goal of building a large indigenous retail brand that provides groceries, household items, health and beauty products, baked goods, and other everyday necessities in a one stop shopping enviroment.

We sell most of the things you'd expect in a large supermarket

Why Shop at Supersaver?

Wide Product Range – Find groceries, household essentials, personal care items, beverages, and more all in one place.

Affordable Prices – Competitive pricing helps customers get value for their money.

Quality Products – Stocked with trusted brands and carefully selected products.
